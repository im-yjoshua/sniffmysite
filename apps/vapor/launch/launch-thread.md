**1/**
I spent my weekend building a lab that sniffs AI startups. Yes, really.

Paste a URL, get a score: 0 = smells fine, 100 = pure vapor.

Meet SniffMySite. 🧪

**2/**
Every AI startup is "revolutionizing" something. We read the landing page — never the product, never the people — and run six smell checks:

- hype words
- big claims with no proof
- empty sentences
- sketchy testimonials
- hidden prices
- stale pages

**3/**
0 smells fine. 100 is pure vapor.

Each check scores 0–100. The two biggest checks count the most. They blend into one score — and the same page always gets the same score.

**4/**
Then the lab slams a rubber stamp on the report:

CERTIFIED REAL. MOSTLY HARMLESS. SUS. Or — for the worst offenders — PURE UNCUT VAPOR.

The stamp is the loudest thing on the page. On purpose.

**5/**
Every result gets a share card: the score, the stamp, the six checks, and one plain-English sentence for each one.

Sniff a startup. Post the card. Start a fight. 📋

**6/**
The integrity line: nobody can pay to hide a bad score.

Paying only buys you a re-test — and a re-test can score you *worse*. The leaderboard keeps receipts.

**7/**
Already live in the lab: scans, a leaderboard of 20 startups we've already sniffed, report pages, claim-your-page with a DNS check, and share cards.

Honest status: not public yet — payments are still in test mode and the domain isn't bought. Link drops when it's real.

**8/**
